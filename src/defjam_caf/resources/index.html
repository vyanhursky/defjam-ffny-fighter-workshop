Fighter outfits

Face presets

Original-game facial choices. Changes are staged for Save and support Undo/Reset. Imported atlases must be cleared or reimported after changing appearance.

Advanced · face proportions (experimental)

Standalone projects only. Sliders replace the previous slider sculpt. Inspect eyes, hair, earrings and neck fit. Save sculpts in your project and export with a CAF v4 runtime. Windows gameplay has a bounded pilot test; broad facial-animation and accessory-fit qualification is pending.

Expert · sculpted head (experimental)

Sculpt the exported head with vertex order, triangulation, UVs and neck seam preserved. Keep native orientation and scale; do not weld, remesh, subdivide or apply transforms. The game retains its rig, textures and normals. Arbitrary scanned meshes require retopology onto this template.
