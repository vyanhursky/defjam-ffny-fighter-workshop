Your own artwork.

Paint this outfit with your own images. Appearance chooses which of the game's clothes, face and body are worn; Textures repaints them. Everything here belongs to the outfit chosen above the viewer.

Selector portrait

Stored in the project. Export turns it into the in-game selector portraits and thumbnails. Titlecards, loading art and icons are under Menu artwork.

Body & clothing artwork

Each sheet paints one part of the fighter. Importing a sheet replaces the game's original art for that part only; every other part keeps the original. A sheet is a square PNG (256, 512, 1024 or 2048 pixels) with the game's original layout. The game keeps its original transparency, and imported colors stay when you change body, face or clothes.

Details & export.

Name your fighter, then export it as a mod. Story progress and currency stay in your profile.

The donor supplies voice, AI and inherited presentation. CAF appearance and fighting-style combination come from this project. Changing stable IDs creates a different mod identity.

Outfits

Switch outfits with the selector above the viewer. Up to three per fighter; stats, moves and voice are shared.

The first export asks for your game checkout and remembers it. The whole pack is checked against your game files before it is saved. To play it, open Mods > Fighters in the launcher, add the ZIP and enable it.

Menu artwork.

Import portraits, titlecards and icons for the selector, loading and result screens. Keep transparent PNGs at their original resolution.

The UI donor supplies original layouts and missing artwork. It does not change your fighter's voice or moves. Retail import stages 24 PNGs from your own game files.

Default-outfit UI art is shared unless another outfit overrides a role. Advanced entries target one 2-, 3- or 4-player layout. Native material/quad layouts stay intact; HD PNG tiles load automatically with the fighter. Import stages changes; Save project writes them. Undo and Reset cover the complete project.

Make it yours.

Pick the game's own body, face and clothes; try items, colors and fits as you browse. To paint your own artwork onto them, use the Textures tab.

Face

Original-game facial choices. Changes are staged for Save and support Undo/Reset. Imported PNG artwork stays applied across these changes.

Standalone projects only. Sliders replace the previous slider sculpt. Inspect eyes, hair, earrings and neck fit. Sculpts are saved in your project and need a game build with face-sculpt support. Tested on Windows only so far: check eyes, hair and accessories in the game.

Sculpt the exported head with vertex order, triangulation, UVs and neck seam preserved. Keep native orientation and scale; do not weld, remesh, subdivide or apply transforms. The game retains its rig, textures and normals. Arbitrary scanned meshes require retopology onto this template.

Clothing & accessories

Selections preview automatically. Equip adds the item to Pending Changes; Save writes the profile. Poses, lighting and No clothes are temporary preview controls.
